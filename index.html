?FAQ

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Our concierge replies within minutes, day or night.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2 PM and check-out is until noon. Early check-in and late check-out are free for Suite guests and on request for everyone else.

Is the pool and spa included?

Yes. The infinity pool, saunas, hammam and fitness studio on Floor 12 are free for all hotel guests. Treatments are booked separately.

Can I visit the Sky Bar without staying?

Absolutely. Cloud 60 is open to the public from 5 PM. Hotel guests get priority seating and can reserve sunset window tables.

Do you offer parking and airport transfer?

Valet parking is $45 per night with EV charging. Our limousine to the airport takes about 25 minutes and costs $65 one way.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival on all direct bookings. After that, the first night is charged.

Are pets and children welcome?

Kids under 12 stay free in their parents' room, with cribs on request. Small dogs are welcome on Floors 15–29 for $40 per stay.
